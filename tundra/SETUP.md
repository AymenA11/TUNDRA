# TUNDRA store activation
The private preview is a caffeine-only redesign; its three product slots replace nicotine products pending access to the source catalog. No Base44 runtime or badge is used.

Before enabling purchases confirm product names, pack sizes, caffeine content, ingredients, directions, warnings, prices, stock, business contact details, delivery charges, returns and privacy policies. Replace illustrative product copy and packaging with accurate approved assets. This store does not claim to treat nicotine dependence.

Create the merchant's Stripe account and verified Products/Prices. Configure STRIPE_SECRET_KEY and STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET as Sites secrets. Set CATALOG_JSON to a JSON object mapping mint, citrus, berry to {"priceId":"price_..."}. Prices are trusted server-side and never accepted from the browser. Configure shipping/tax appropriately in Stripe and the checkout endpoint before launch. Enable STORE_ENABLED=true only after replacing preview labels with the confirmed catalog and testing.

Register /api/stripe/webhook with Stripe for checkout.session.completed and checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded. Paid sessions are recorded idempotently in D1; pending or failed payments are not recorded as paid. Stripe retains customer/order details. Shipping fulfillment, inventory management, refunds and customer support are managed in Stripe/manual operations for this first version; no admin dashboard or automated fulfillment is included.

Start with test-mode keys and test a successful card payment, cancellation, declined payment, asynchronous success, invalid signature and duplicate webhook. The current private audience prevents Stripe calling the webhook; public store access must be enabled intentionally before end-to-end webhook testing and launch. No real payment has been tested or enabled.

## Launch promotion and account access
The popup displays a proposed WELCOME10 10% first-order launch offer, explicitly marked as a preview. Before launch create the Stripe promotion with first-time transaction restrictions, 10% coupon, once duration, redemption/expiry terms chosen by the merchant; set STRIPE_WELCOME_PROMOTION_ID. Checkout uses only this configured promotion ID and rejects unconfigured offers. Change popup copy to the final confirmed terms before enabling sales. Saving an offer is local to the visitor's device and collects no email address.

/account uses Sites ChatGPT identity and its supported sign-in flow. Email/password, Google and Apple customer authentication are not separately implemented. Order history remains unavailable until the store and customer mapping are configured.

Four VELO 0 mg product photos were downloaded from the Snus Vikings public product catalogue; local filenames map to exact zero-nicotine products. ZYN Zero photos were not found there, so those cards retain labelled illustrations. Confirm image reuse rights before public commercial launch.

## Age checks, payments and shipping preparation
The 18+ browsing gate is a session-only self-declaration. It is not identity verification. Payment requires sign-in and a server-side age check linked to the signed-in user. Stripe Identity document + matching-selfie checks are prepared but disabled until AGE_VERIFICATION_ENABLED=true, a Stripe secret key and a restricted results key are set. The restricted key needs Identity Verification Results and Recent Detailed Verification Results Read. Verified DOB is used only in memory to calculate 18+; the app stores the Stripe verification session reference, result expiry and test/live mode, not DOB or ID images. Results expire after 24 hours. Test-mode results cannot authorize live payments. Confirm your privacy notice, Stripe Identity eligibility and approved verification flow before enabling it.

Checkout requires STRIPE_SHIPPING_RATE_ID, a confirmed CATALOG_JSON of approved Price IDs, a matching Stripe webhook signing secret, and STORE_ENABLED=true. Placeholder IDs are rejected server-side. Shipping is GB-only. Customer email comes from signed-in identity; delivery details are collected by Stripe Checkout. If WELCOME10 is saved it must be configured or checkout rejects it. Stripe handles card entry; this app does not store card numbers. Product tax configuration must be confirmed in Stripe before launch.

Paid Stripe checkout events are signature-checked, and store an order plus a fulfillment record atomically. Each Stripe Session ID is unique, so duplicate webhooks do not create duplicate orders. Pending/failed payments are not released. Records contain the recipient name/address and line items needed for shipping; no public endpoint exposes these records. The order-status endpoint checks the signed-in owner of the Stripe session. Do not fulfill from the browser return page.

Royal Mail is NOT yet connected. Fulfillment records currently have status awaiting_shipping_setup. A future Click & Drop API integration can create an order after verified payment, using a stable reference for reconciliation, the delivery address and correct package weights/service. Carrier order creation, postage payment, label retrieval, collection booking, retries/reconciliation, cancellation/refund handling and tracking are not implemented in this version. You must choose and connect a Click & Drop account/service and provide parcel dimensions, weight and dispatch address before that integration is activated. Pack orders, print/apply labels, and arrange collection or drop-off. Creating an electronic order does not dispatch a physical parcel.

Before live activation: run end-to-end test-mode payments and Identity checks; test under-18/missing DOB, declined/cancelled/asynchronous payments, invalid and duplicate webhooks, owner-only status access, and confirm fulfillment details. Because the Site is currently owner-private, Stripe cannot deliver webhooks until public access is intentionally configured. No payment, ID verification, or shipment has been performed during development.

## Full-range expansion and strength hover overlay
The catalogue now includes every individual pouch entry in the four downloaded Snus Vikings brand feeds (excluding one bundle, preserving zero-product aliases), supplemented from all six observed NGP manufacturer catalogue pages, the VELO UK product/variant feed, and ZYN UK's published structured Product records. Manufacturer supplement duplicates and one beverage were removed. This is a dated snapshot of those accessible catalogues, not a claim to cover every regional, discontinued or unreleased SKU worldwide. Catalogue provenance, source counts and unresolved fields are in catalogue-coverage.json.

Every original listing keeps its category and contents. Unknown per-pouch nicotine amounts remain null. The hover overlay explicitly shows unconfirmed rather than deriving a strength from a mg/g-only label or guessing from title text. Nicotine strength score is round(nicotine mg/pouch divided by the maximum confirmed nicotine mg/pouch in this catalogue times 100); currently 100 means 30 mg/pouch. Caffeine uses its own separately labelled maximum; the two scores are not comparable. These are catalogue comparison scores, not manufacturer ratings, safety ratings or absorbed doses. Changing the catalogue maximum changes the score basis; exact contents stay visible on cards and product pages.

The overlay fades/slides over images on hover and keyboard focus, then animates the bar to its value. On touch layouts it remains visible. Reduced-motion disables transitions. Collection pages show 24 products initially with a Show more control; searching and filtering reset that limit. All imported products remain accessible through collection search and their own detail pages. No live payment, stock or availability was enabled.
